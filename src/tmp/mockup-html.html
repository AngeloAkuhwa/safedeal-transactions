Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the SafeDeal buyer process

What happens after I pay?

Your payment is held securely by SafeDeal. The transaction agreement becomes locked, and the seller is notified to begin fulfillment. You'll receive tracking information once the item ships.

What if the item doesn't match?

You have 72 hours after delivery to verify the item. If it doesn't match this agreement, you can raise a dispute with evidence. SafeDeal will review and mediate fairly.

When does the seller receive payment?

The seller receives payment only after you confirm the item matches the agreement, or after the 72-hour verification window expires without a dispute.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes. SafeDeal uses bank-level 256-bit SSL encryption and is PCI DSS compliant. Your payment information is never shared with the seller.
